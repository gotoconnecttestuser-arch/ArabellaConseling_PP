How We Use SMS Messaging
Arabella Counseling uses SMS messaging to communicate with clients regarding:

Information We Collect
When you opt in to receive SMS messages, we may collect:
- Your mobile phone number
- Your name and contact information
- Message content and communication preferences
- Appointment-related information necessary for client communication

How We Use Your Information
We use your information solely to:
- Provide counseling-related customer care communications
- Respond to inquiries and support requests
- Send appointment and service notifications
- Improve our communication processes and client experience
- Maintain records required for operational and legal purposes

SMS Consent and Data Sharing
SMS consent is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing use.

We may share information with service providers only as necessary to operate our messaging services, scheduling systems, or as otherwise required by law.

Opt-In
Clients may opt in to receive SMS messages by:
- Providing verbal consent
- Completing intake or consent forms
- Submitting a web form
- Requesting SMS communication directly

Message Frequency
Message frequency may vary depending on your appointments, requests, and ongoing service needs.
